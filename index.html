Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Clean Launcher support Chromecast with Google TV and Fire TV sticks?

Yes. Clean Launcher runs on any device powered by Android TV OS (Android 7.0 and up), including Google TV Chromecast, Xiaomi Mi Box, Sony Bravia, TCL, Philips, and Amazon Fire OS devices via standard sideloading.

How does Clean Launcher keep its file size down to ~2MB?

By removing third-party telemetry, ad frameworks, and heavy Material components. Everything is built with standard native Android Views and hardware-accelerated GPU textures.

Can I switch back to my TV's original default launcher anytime?

Yes. Clean Launcher includes an educational 3-step onboarding flow and a direct "Exit Launcher" button that immediately routes you back to your TV's Default Apps settings. You are never locked in.

Why does Clean Launcher need Usage Access and Location permissions?

Usage Access allows the launcher to calculate your 7-day most-used streaming apps and sort them to the front. Location permission is required by Android to read your active Wi-Fi SSID name. No information ever leaves your device.
